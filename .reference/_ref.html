Six Core Capabilities of PrintXpand Connect

Single API to 12+ Vendors

One integration connects your eCommerce store to SanMar, S&S Activewear, MidOcean, PF Concept, and more. No individual vendor integrations to build or maintain — PrintXpand Connect handles all vendor APIs under one unified interface.

Real-Time Inventory Sync

Live inventory feeds from every connected vendor. Your eCommerce store shows only what's actually available — updated in real time. No more selling products that are out of stock. Inventory alerts when stock drops below thresholds.

Automated Purchase Orders

When a customer places an order, PrintXpand Connect generates purchase orders to the appropriate vendor automatically. No manual re-entry, no email orders, no phone calls. Order → PO → vendor fulfillment in one automated flow.

24-Hour Vendor Onboarding

Add a new vendor's entire catalog — products, images, pricing, and inventory — with pre-built connections. PrintXpand Connect handles SKU mapping, category alignment, and data normalisation automatically. No spreadsheet imports.

Margin-Protected Pricing

Set markup rules per vendor, per product category, or per customer segment. PrintXpand Connect applies your pricing rules on top of vendor cost — margins are protected automatically as vendor prices change.

Drop-Ship Fulfillment

Products ship directly from the vendor to your customer — you never touch the product. PrintXpand Connect manages tracking numbers, shipping notifications, and delivery updates across all vendor shipments.

How It Works

Connect Your Vendor Network in 3 Steps

1

Multiple Suppliers

SanMar, S&S Activewear, PF Concept, Solo by MidOcean and 8 more pre-integrated vendor catalogs feed into a single PrintXpand Connect endpoint. No per-vendor APIs to build, no SKU mapping by hand — every supplier in the network is normalised on day one.

PXConnect Engine

Real-time stock, pricing, product data and order webhooks all flow through one engine. Margin rules, vendor routing logic and decoration specs live in one place. Your Storefront

Drop-ship fulfilment lands the product on your customer's doorstep without you ever holding stock. Tracking numbers, shipping notifications and delivery updates flow back into your storefront automatically — closing the loop end to end.
